Kazufumi TSUBOI (坪井 和史)

  • Welcome to my homepage!
    I am Kazufumi TSUBOI(坪井 和史), an Associate Professor at the Department of Economics, Nagoya University of Economics, Japan.
    My research interests include Urban Economics, Human Mobility, Economic Policy, and City's Self-Organization.
    I am also a visiting researcher at the Graduate School of Information Science, Tohoku University, Japan.
